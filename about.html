Origins

Our Story

FitNest started in 2026 when our founder, a certified personal trainer, noticed a gap: millions of Indians wanted to get fit but couldn't commit to gym schedules, expensive memberships, or long commutes.

We built FitNest to solve exactly that. By bringing expert trainers directly into your home through live video sessions, we've helped many clients across India transform their health — on their own terms.

Today, FitNest is a growing community of dedicated coaches and motivated clients who believe that consistency, not perfection, is the key to lasting fitness.

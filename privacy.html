FITNEST

MEMBERSHIP POLICY & TERMS OF SERVICE

Welcome to Fitnest!

We are committed to providing quality fitness coaching, personalised guidance and a positive training experience. To ensure clarity and fairness for all members, please read the following membership policies carefully before starting your programme.

1 FITNESS ASSESSMENT & REGISTRATION

  • Every member must complete the Fitnest Fitness Assessment Form before starting training.
  • Members must provide accurate details about their medical history, existing health conditions, injuries and fitness goals.
  • Any changes in health conditions or new injuries must be communicated to the trainer promptly.

2 TRAINING PACKAGES & MEMBERSHIP VALIDITY

A. Individual Personal Training, Couple Training & Duo Training

  • All three packages include 22 training sessions within 35 calendar days.
  • Membership expires upon completion of 22 sessions or 35 calendar days, whichever occurs first.
  • Couple and Duo Training sessions are shared between two registered participants.
  • If one participant attends and the other is absent, the session will count as completed for both participants.

B. Group Training

  • 22 training sessions within 30 calendar days.
  • Sessions are conducted Monday to Friday, with Saturday and Sunday as weekly holidays.
  • Missed sessions cannot be carried forward or compensated, except where an extension is approved under the exceptional circumstances policy.

3 TRAINING SCHEDULE, SESSION DURATION & RESCHEDULING

  • Each training session will last approximately 45 minutes to 1 hour, depending on the training programme and session requirements.
  • Members are expected to follow their mutually agreed training schedule.
  • If the trainer is unable to conduct a scheduled session, members will be informed in advance. The session will be rescheduled at a mutually convenient time without deducting it from the membership package.
  • For Couple and Duo Training, if both participants are unable to attend, at least 12 hours’ advance notice is required to request rescheduling.
  • Rescheduling requests are subject to trainer availability.
  • Late cancellations or missed sessions without sufficient notice may be counted as completed sessions.

4 MEDICAL & EXCEPTIONAL CIRCUMSTANCES

  • Members enrolled in Personal Training or Group Training who cannot attend for 7 or more consecutive days due to illness, injury or genuine personal circumstances may request a membership extension.
  • Members should inform Fitnest in advance whenever possible, preferably before the absence begins.
  • Supporting medical documents may be requested where necessary.
  • Each request will be reviewed individually and is subject to Fitnest approval.

5 PAYMENT & MEMBERSHIP RENEWAL

  • Membership fees must be paid in full before the training programme begins.
  • Membership validity starts from the mutually agreed activation date.
  • Renewal payments must be completed before starting the next membership package.
  • Memberships are non-transferable and cannot be assigned to another person.
  • Additional sessions beyond the selected package may involve extra charges.
  • Unused sessions expire at the end of the membership validity period unless an extension is approved by Fitnest.

6 MEMBER RESPONSIBILITIES & FITNESS DISCLAIMER

  • Members must provide accurate health information and communicate any relevant changes to the trainer.
  • Any pain, dizziness or unusual discomfort during exercise must be reported immediately.
  • Members are expected to follow the trainer’s instructions and maintain a safe training environment.
  • Fitness results vary depending on individual factors, consistency, nutrition and recovery. Fitnest does not guarantee specific weight-loss, muscle-gain or body-transformation results.
  • Fitness training is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

7 ONLINE TRAINING & MEETING ACCESS

  • The official meeting link will be shared after successful payment and membership confirmation.
  • Members must use the designated meeting app to join their scheduled training sessions.
  • The same meeting link will be used for all sessions throughout the membership validity period.
  • Members must join on time and must not share the meeting link with others.

OUR COMMITMENT

At Fitnest, we believe fitness should fit into your lifestyle. Our commitment is to provide accessible, effective and sustainable fitness coaching through quality training, personalised guidance and continuous support.

We look forward to supporting you as you work towards a healthier, stronger and fitter lifestyle.

THANK YOU FOR CHOOSING FITNEST.

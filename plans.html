Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pause my membership if I travel?

Yes! You can pause your plan for up to 14 days per quarter with a simple WhatsApp message to your coach.

Do I need equipment to start?

Not at all. Over 80% of our beginners start with just a yoga mat and their own bodyweight. When you're ready, coaches guide you on simple resistance bands or dumbbells.

What happens during the free trial session?

You will meet 1-on-1 on Google Meet / Zoom with our coach, do a gentle 15-minute mobility & strength screen, and discuss your fitness goals.

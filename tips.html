5 Easy High-Protein Meals You Can Make in 15 Minutes

Fuel your workouts without spending hours in the kitchen. Getting adequate daily protein supports muscle retention, accelerates fat reduction, and keeps hunger stable throughout the workday.

1. Low-Fat Paneer & Bell Pepper Toss (36g Protein)

Cube 200g low-fat malai paneer. Sauté in half a teaspoon of olive oil with sliced peppers, onions, jeera, and rock salt. Ready in 8 minutes.

2. Spiced Moong & Peanut Chaat (20g Clean Plant Protein)

Mix steamed green moong sprouts with diced cucumber, onions, tomatoes, freshly squeezed lime, and roasted peanuts for crunch.

3. Masala Egg Bhurji on Multigrain Toast (24g Protein)

Whisk 3 eggs with chopped green chilies, coriander, onions, and turmeric. Scramble lightly and serve on two slices of whole wheat sourdough.

Why Sleep Is Your Most Powerful Recovery Tool

Your muscles don't grow while lifting; they repair and grow during deep sleep. Discover how optimizing your rest delivers 2x the fitness results.

During deep non-REM stage 3 sleep, blood supply to your muscles increases significantly, delivering extra oxygen and nutrients that facilitate tissue repair and cell regeneration.

Sleep deprivation also elevates cortisol (the stress hormone) which prompts visceral belly fat storage and breaks down lean muscle tissue.
